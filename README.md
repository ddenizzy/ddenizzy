## Hi, I'm Deniz 

Industrial and Systems Engineering student in the **SUNY Binghamton – Istanbul Technical University dual diploma program** (expected May 2027). I like turning messy, everyday problems into working systems, from optimization models to tools I use every day.

### What I'm working on

- **[OCC Bus Reliability Study](https://github.com/ddenizzy/occ-reliability):** how on time are Binghamton's campus buses? A 24/7 logger on a small cloud server records every arrival from the live feed to measure on-time performance, bunching and real rider wait by route, hour and stop.
- **[MasterPrac](https://github.com/ddenizzy/masterprac):** a macOS guitar practice app that syncs Songsterr tabs, a live fretboard and word-timed lyrics to whatever is playing in Spotify.
- **OutbreakLens** (senior design capstone): a disease outbreak forecasting pipeline that combines an SEIR model with machine learning on public WHO and CDC data.
- **[Custamo](https://github.com/ddenizzy/custamo-app):** a personal macOS menu bar assistant with a retro pixel look for mail, deadlines, tasks, a kitchen inventory, campus buses and smart lights.
